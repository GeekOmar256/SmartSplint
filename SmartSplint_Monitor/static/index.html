Calibration is saved on this computer and used automatically every time the app starts. Repeat it whenever the splint is refitted or a sensor is replaced.

🔄 Motion sensors (IMU)

  1. Fit the splint and stick both IMU boxes to the skin.
  2. Rest the arm on a table in the normal position.
  3. Press Start and keep completely still for 3 seconds.
